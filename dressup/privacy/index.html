DressUp: Fashion Stylist — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This policy describes how the game DressUp: Fashion Stylist (package com.adaindiegames.DressUp) by AdaIndieGames handles information. In short: the game does not collect, store on our side or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts and no sign-up. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no other third-party services that collect data. It does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Information stored on your device

Your saved looks and your settings (sound, music, language) are stored only on your device. We have no access to them. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Sharing your looks

When you tap Share, the game creates a picture of your look on your device and opens the system share sheet. You choose where the picture goes. We do not receive it.

Internet

The game does not send any data over the internet. The “Rate the game” button only opens the game's page in Google Play.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
